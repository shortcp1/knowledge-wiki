### Marketing AI Leadership Gap & Displacement Anxiety (Lomanto/Writer, June 2026)
- **Business Problem**: Employee displacement anxiety undermining AI transformation effectiveness despite high adoption rates
- **Industry/Function**: Marketing (generalizable to knowledge work)
- **Quantitative Findings (2026 AI Adoption in the Enterprise Survey)**:
  - **43%** of marketing employees using AI believe company would replace them with AI agent immediately "regardless of years of service or loyalty"
  - **53%** of executives cite "efficiency with leaner team" as 3-year AI success metric
  - **47%** of executives say "productivity without adding headcount" is primary AI investment driver
  - **58%** of employees report manager is "open to AI" but gives little real direction/encouragement
  - **55%** of marketing employees claim more AI role knowledge than their direct manager
  - **35%** have manager who actively champions AI (only)
  - **25.8%** believe openly criticizing company AI approach is career risk
  - **26%+** believe refusing to use AI would result in termination
  - **72%** of C-suite leaders report company AI strategy is source of stress
- **Organizational Failure Pattern**: Cost-reduction narrative dominates without articulated growth/value-creation strategy; creates "silent compliance" where resistance looks like agreement
- **Manager Competence Crisis**: Credibility gap when managers lack hands-on AI tool experience in their specific function
- **Success Pattern**: Leaders who articulate human-centered, revenue-focused vision before efficiency narrative dominates; clear role redefinition in AI-augmented state
- **Generalizability**: Pattern applies across all knowledge work functions where executive metrics focus on headcount reduction rather than capability expansion
