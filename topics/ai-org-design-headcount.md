## AI-Native Organizational Structure

### Management Layer Changes
- **Flatter hierarchies**: AI-native firms have fewer managers of humans compared to non-AI-native organizations (empirical observation, source: Exponential View research, June 2026)
- **Precision clarification**: Reduction specifically in "managers of humans" rather than elimination of management function itself
- **Role transformation**: Every frontier employee becomes "a manager of agentic teams" (source: Azeem Azhar, June 2026)
  - This represents shift in management scope rather than reduction in management activity
  - Management capability becomes distributed to individual contributors rather than concentrated in dedicated management roles

### Implications
- Flatter org charts may mask equivalent or increased management workload, now distributed across workforce
- Requires new skillsets: managing AI agents vs. managing human teams
- May have implications for career progression paths and organizational design principles
