# AI Org Design & Headcount

Tracks how organizations are restructuring teams, roles, and reporting structures in response to AI adoption. Covers the emergence of new roles (AI engineers, product managers) and the elimination or transformation of existing ones.

## AI-Native Startup Structure

Research from Harvard Business School (Koning) and INSEAD (Kim, 2026) comparing AI-native startups to traditional startups at similar funding and growth stages found:

- **25% smaller headcount** in AI-native companies
- **Higher engineer density** - more engineers relative to total headcount
- **Denser expertise concentration** - specialized technical knowledge concentrated in smaller teams
- **Fewer managers** - flatter organizational structures with reduced management layers

### Mechanism: Product-Layer Integration

The structural difference stems from AI being integrated directly into the product layer rather than deployed as edge tools for human workers. Knowledge work previously performed by human teams at organizational edges is now executed within the product itself, eliminating the need for those human roles.

Key insight: "Capturing real value from AI often requires re-engineering processes around it—and, as our results suggest, re-engineering the product itself so that AI does the work directly." This creates tighter feedback loops and reduces coordination overhead.

### Startup Capability Transformation

Sam Altman (Stanford CS153, 2026): "With an affordable amount of spend on tokens, you can do what a 100 person incredibly great engineering team would do as a startup." This represents a fundamental shift in:
- Startup ambition scope
- Development velocity
- Parallel workstream capacity
