## Economic Pressures on Foundation Model Labs

### Bear Case Economics (Thompson, June 2026)

**Value Capture Problem**:
- First few years of AI: compute captured most economic value (Nvidia, TSMC, memory makers)
- Labs (Anthropic, OpenAI): Collectively lost tens of billions building frontier models
- Distillation vulnerability: Released models rapidly commoditized by open source (primarily Chinese labs)
- Article truncated mid-analysis of bear case

### Amazon-Anthropic Infrastructure Dynamics

**Critical Dependency**:
- Amazon serves dual role: major investor + major inference provider to Anthropic
- Amazon reportedly identified/reported jailbreak that triggered government action (June 2026)
- Complexity: Infrastructure provider monitoring customer's model security creates unique governance dynamics
