### Government Relations and Policy Advocacy (2026)

**Anti-Distillation Campaign**:
- Led campaign targeting Chinese AI companies through blog posts and letters to government representatives
- Shared policy recommendations regarding foreign company use of Anthropic API
- **Technical evidence disclosure**: Minimal technical evidence shared publicly to support claims
- **API usage detection**: Identified foreign companies accessing and paying for Anthropic API, subsequently terminated access
- **Criticism**: Campaign characterized by external observers as [[regulatory-policy|regulatory capture]], with Anthropic standing to gain substantial economic security if accused Chinese competitors were banned
- **API security question**: Critics note apparent contradiction between claims of model power requiring restrictions and inability to technically secure API against unauthorized distillation

**Economic Incentives**: If Chinese model makers accused by Anthropic were banned, Anthropic would gain substantial competitive advantage and economic security for its products.
