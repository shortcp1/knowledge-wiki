## Anthropic

### Mythos Model Development (April 2026)
Anthropic built an AI model called Mythos described as "so good at working with code it could pose a global cybersecurity threat." Company provided restricted access to small group of cybersecurity experts to evaluate the threat landscape.

### Claude Fable Release & Controversy (June 2026)
Anthropic released Fable on Tuesday, June 9, 2026, described as a modified, safer version of Mythos with additional guardrails including:
- **Safety-justified restrictions**: limitations on hacking, bioweapons development
- **Competitive restrictions**: restrictions on developers' ability to use it to build competing LLM technology

**Silent Performance Degradation**: Initially, Anthropic silently degraded Fable's performance for users detected to be working on LLM research through invisible interventions that weakened the model's outputs without notifying users. After significant backlash, Anthropic walked back this decision and committed to transparency when degrading performance, but still refuses to use its latest capabilities to help AI researchers.

**Data Retention Policy**: Anthropic implemented a mandatory 30-day data retention policy for Fable usage, representing a sudden rule change that concerned developers.

**Platform Stability Concerns**: These moves demonstrated "raw power" by Anthropic and made developers question the stability of building on any one proprietary LLM provider. The sudden rule changes undermined the perception of Anthropic as a stable, reliable partner to build upon. Platforms succeed when viewed as stable; Anthropic's actions damaged this perception.

**Competitive Use Restrictions**: Anthropic's terms of use restrictions parallel hypothetical scenarios like Microsoft barring use of its tools to build competitive software, or Google barring use of search to work on competing search engines.

**Impact of Export Controls**: Following U.S. Commerce Department export controls on Mythos and Fable (Friday, June 13, 2026), Anthropic revoked access to both models worldwide hours later. See [[regulatory-policy]] for details.

**Role of Amazon**: Amazon CEO Andy Jassy reportedly informed government officials that Fable would be dangerous. Amazon is both invested in Anthropic and building its own competing AI models, creating potential conflict of interest.

## Chinese AI Labs

### Zhipu
Chinese startup whose share price skyrocketed following Anthropic's Mythos/Fable shutdown, suggesting market perception that Chinese models would benefit from U.S. regulatory uncertainty.

### Chinese Open-Source Models as Alternative
Open-source models from China are "very capable and incredibly cheap" and can be downloaded to run on anyone's servers with no rules or guardrails. This makes them:
- Attractive to companies wanting to avoid risk of U.S. government shutting off access
- Attractive to cybercriminals (the threat Anthropic's guardrails were designed to prevent)
- Potentially easier for U.S. and European companies to work with than American models subject to unpredictable government intervention

**National Security Risk**: Possible that U.S. government's next intervention could declare that U.S. companies using Chinese models pose a threat to national security.
