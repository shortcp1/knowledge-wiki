## Emergency National Security Measures

**Foreign National Access Suspension (June 2025)**
U.S. government issued export control directive invoking national security authorities to suspend all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 by any foreign national, whether inside or outside the United States, including foreign national employees at Anthropic. Directive was issued with minimal notice (5:21pm ET notification) and required abrupt disabling of models for all customers to ensure compliance. The letter did not provide specific details of national security concerns.

**Post-Release Model Suspension**
This represents the first documented case of a publicly released model being suspended via national security directive after [[jailbreak-vulnerability|jailbreak discovery]]. The government's action was triggered by becoming aware of a method to bypass Fable 5's guardrails, demonstrated to identify "a small number of previously known, minor vulnerabilities." 

**Contested Justification**
Anthropics position: The identified vulnerabilities were relatively simple and discoverable by other publicly-available models without requiring jailbreaks; non-universal jailbreaks are inevitable and narrow in scope; no evidence of universal jailbreak existed. White House position: Anthropic leadership showed insouciance to legitimate national security concerns.

## Infrastructure Provider Monitoring

**Amazon Reporting to Government**
Amazon reported the Fable 5 [[cybersecurity-jailbreaks|jailbreak]] to the U.S. government, despite being both an investor in and major inference provider to Anthropic. This suggests infrastructure providers may have monitoring obligations or voluntary reporting relationships regarding models deployed on their platforms. The dynamics between commercial relationships and national security reporting remain unclear.

## Model Capability Thresholds for Intervention

**Dynamic Threshold Problem**
Government intervention may not require current models to meet absolute capability thresholds; the expectation that subsequent model generations will cross thresholds appears sufficient to trigger preemptive action. This is particularly relevant as [[lab-dynamics|models become increasingly useful in creating their successors]], suggesting an acceleration dynamic in capability development.
