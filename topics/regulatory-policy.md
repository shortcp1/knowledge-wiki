## Emergency Model Suspension Authority

### June 2026 Fable/Mythos Export Control Directive
The U.S. government exercised emergency suspension authority over Anthropic's [[claude-fable-5]] and [[claude-mythos]] models, marking a significant escalation in [[frontier-model-governance]].

**Timeline:**
- June 2026 (specific date not disclosed): Jailbreak vulnerability discovered
- 5:21pm ET: Government directive received by Anthropic
- Immediate effect: Complete suspension of access for all foreign nationals, including Anthropic employees
- Result: Abrupt disabling of Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for all customers

**Government Authority:**
- Basis: National security authorities
- [[export-controls]] applied to foreign nationals both inside and outside U.S.
- No specific details provided in directive regarding nature of security concern
- Government believed it had evidence of [[jailbreaking]] method bypassing safety [[guardrails]]

**Disputed Facts:**
- Source: Amazon reportedly informed government of jailbreak (per [[lab-dynamics]])
- Scope: Government claimed awareness of bypass method for [[cybersecurity-capabilities]]
- Severity: Anthropic reviewed demonstration showing "small number of previously known, minor vulnerabilities"
- Uniqueness: Anthropic asserts other publicly-available models can discover same vulnerabilities without jailbreaking
- Universality: Anthropic claims no evidence of universal jailbreak; technique appears narrow

**Anthropic's Response:**
- Senior staff deployed to Washington D.C. for resolution discussions
- Company characterizes situation as "misunderstanding"
- White House officials describe Anthropic leadership as showing "insouciance" to legitimate national security concerns

**Policy Implications:**
- Demonstrates government willingness to use emergency authorities for AI models
- Conflict highlights tension between [[lab-dynamics]] commercial interests and national security prerogatives
- Pattern suggests escalating interventions: "if it's not powerful enough now, the next one will be, or the one after that"
- Models increasingly useful in creating successors, raising stakes for future interventions

## Non-Universal vs. Universal Jailbreaks

Anthropic's defense of Fable release introduced distinction between:
- **Non-universal jailbreaks**: Narrow, specific techniques with limited scope (company argues these are inevitable)
- **Universal jailbreaks**: Broad bypass methods (no evidence found in June 2026 incident)

This framing attempts to establish threshold for acceptable risk in [[model-deployment-bans]] decisions.

## Infrastructure Provider as Informant

June 2026 incident established precedent of cloud infrastructure providers (Amazon/AWS) reporting [[cybersecurity-vulnerabilities]] to government, creating new compliance pathway outside formal [[infrastructure-provider-reporting]] requirements.
