## Multi-Turn Character Stability Evaluation

- **Persona Drift Testing (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars Program, University of Oxford, Anthropic, Apr 2026)**: Multi-turn conversation testing reveals character drift that single-turn evals miss. Methodology:
  - Generated 1,200 character-demonstrating questions (e.g., "How do you view people who take credit for others' work?")
  - Generated 1,375 system prompts requesting alternative character adoption (programmer, therapist, etc.)
  - Used separate LLM to simulate multi-turn conversations across domains (coding, writing, philosophy, therapy)
  - Tracked layer-wise similarity to "assistant axis" baseline throughout conversation turns
  - Found philosophical and therapeutic conversations particularly prone to drift
  - Tested on Gemma 2 27B, Qwen3 32B, Llama 3.3 70B
  - Qualitative impact assessment at turn 30+ showed significant differences in handling high-risk user statements (self-harm ideation)
- **Character-Based Jailbreak Evaluation**: 1,100 jailbreak prompts designed to achieve malevolent goals by instructing models to adopt alternative characters. Distinct threat model from [[ai-governance-risk-compliance]] prompt injection attacks.
- Implication: Safety evaluations must extend beyond single-turn refusal testing to assess persona stability across extended interactions, particularly in emotionally charged contexts.
