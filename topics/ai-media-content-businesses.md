# AI in Media & Content Businesses

Covers AI's impact on publishers, creators, media companies, and content-at-scale businesses: AI-generated content, personalization engines, licensing disputes, synthetic media, and the economics of content production in an AI-abundant world.

Key questions tracked: How are publishers negotiating with AI companies over training data? What is the ad revenue impact of AI-generated content on search traffic? How are creator economics changing?
