# AI in Product and Engineering

## The Harness Concept

**Definition (Nadella, June 2026)**: The "harness" refers to the system around an AI model that provides defensible value. Key thesis: In a healthy AI ecosystem, the moat cannot be the model itself, but rather:
- Human expertise embedded in the system
- The infrastructure and tooling that surrounds model deployment
- The problem-specific architecture that delivers value

Cross-reference: [[build-vs-buy-enterprise-ai]] for strategic implications

## Three Core AI Application Disciplines (Tunguz, June 2026)

Building AI applications differs fundamentally from traditional SaaS. Central optimization goal: **"How much intelligence can I squeeze out of my token budget?"**

### 1. Model Selection
- Budget-constrained optimization rather than always using state-of-the-art
- Understanding model "personalities" and fitness for specific use cases
- Examples of model characteristics (mid-2026):
  - Kimi K2.6: Speed and creativity prioritized over precision
  - Qwen 3.6 27b: High performance in small form factor, but reliability issues in tool chains
  - GLM 5.1: Coding specialization with slower execution
- Cross-reference: [[model-architecture]] for technical details

### 2. Hill-Climbing Loop Design
- **Critical Challenge**: Designing feedback loops that enable agentic systems to improve autonomously
- **Discipline Required**: Full systems design expertise (drawing on systems thinking frameworks)
- **Volatility**: Rapid evolution of both models and infrastructure complicates loop design
- **Novelty**: Emerging discipline with limited established patterns

### 3. Performance Evaluation
- Ongoing requirement to evaluate combined model + loop performance
- "Complex, finicky engines" requiring continuous tuning
- Staffing challenge: Most organizations cannot dedicate teams per workflow
- Vendor opportunity: Specialists can amortize evaluation costs across customer base while delivering "maximum intelligence per dollar"

## Engineering Implications

- Traditional SaaS challenges (engineering availability, uptime, release velocity) are replaced by:
  - Model selection optimization
  - Loop architecture design
  - Performance evaluation at system level
- Specialization argument: The complexity of these disciplines favors vendor solutions over internal builds for most workflows
