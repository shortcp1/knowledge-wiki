## Granola: Meeting Context Tool at a16z

### Implementation Details
**Company:** Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)  
**Tool:** Granola  
**Function:** Organizational knowledge and context capture

### Success Factors
"Has better context on a16z's culture, our investments, and how we actually think than almost any other tool we use, because it's been in the room"

**Key capability:** Presence in meetings provides superior cultural and operational context compared to traditional enterprise tools

### Pattern
- Meeting attendance and recording
- Context ingestion about company culture, investment decisions, internal thinking
- Demonstrates value of "living company context" vs static documentation

### Broader Context
Part of emerging trend where "most work discussions are being recorded now by default" and AI agents learn company context through meeting attendance rather than reading documentation alone.

**Related companies adopting similar approaches:**
- OpenAI: Everything recorded, agents stand in for leaders
- Bridgewater: Institutional recording policy
- Shopify: Verbal culture organization
