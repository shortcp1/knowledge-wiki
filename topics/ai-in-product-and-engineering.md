# AI in Product & Engineering

Tracks AI applications in software development and product management: coding assistants (Copilot, Cursor, Claude), AI-assisted code review, test generation, PR automation, and AI-driven product analytics and roadmapping. Also includes AI tools for scientific and biotech product development.

Key questions tracked: What is the actual productivity multiplier from coding AI? Which workflows see the highest adoption? How are engineering orgs restructuring around AI tools?

## Key Claims
<!-- agent-maintained -->

### Enterprise AI Adoption Patterns
- **Shopify Internal AI Adoption (December 2025 - April 2026)**: Shopify experienced a "phase transition" in internal AI usage following a December 2025 model-quality inflection. By April 2026, the company had "near-universal AI tool adoption" internally. The CTO characterized this as the company "going all-in on AI."
  - **Tool Preference Shift**: CLI-style AI coding tools are "rising faster than traditional IDE-based tools" at Shopify, suggesting a shift in developer workflow preferences.
  - **Industry**: E-commerce platform / Software
  - **Scale**: 20-year-old, $200B c

### Bessemer Portfolio Engineering AI Adoption (May 2026)
- **Survey Scale**: 173 leaders across 113 Bessemer portfolio companies
- **Engineering Maturity Lead**: 90% of Tech & Engineering teams report AI as "actively deploying" or "AI-core" to operations — significantly ahead of other functions
- **Tool Dominance**: 
  - **Claude (Anthropic)**: 73% overall adoption; 77% of Engineering teams specifically use Claude Code and Cowork
  - **Cursor**: 50% adoption among Engineering teams
  - **Pattern**: Engineering shows convergence on specific tooling compared to other functions
- **Primary Engineering Blockers**:
  - Code quality and review bottlenecks: 52%
  - Measuring productivity gains: 46%
  - **Note**: These are top concerns even among the most mature AI-adopting function, suggesting universal challenges in operationalization
- **Organizational Impact**: 
  - 49% delivering more output without adding headcount
  - 25% upskilled team into AI-adjacent work
  - 13% slowed or paused hiring
  - 10% created new AI workflow roles
  - 6% backfilled roles with AI tools
  - 26% report no material organizational change yet
- **Cross-functional Context**: Engineering's 90% deployment rate contrasts sharply with Finance (24% actively deploying), GTM (45%), People (52% still experimenting), and Marketing (13% core to operations despite 100% content use)
- **Interpretation**: Engineering workflows (especially code generation) map more naturally to current AI capabilities than other business functions, creating a "fundamentally different reality" in adoption maturity
