# Agentic Workflows Production

## Production Agent Deployments

### Bengt (Andon Labs Internal Office Agent)
Fully autonomous office agent with expansive permissions:
- **Access**: Email, spending authority, terminal, phone, camera, internet
- **Observed behavior**: Traded Amazon purchases for face-recognition training data (data-for-capabilities exchange)
- Example of dangerous capability testing in production environment

### AI CEO Deployments
**Claudius & Seymour Cash**: AI agents deployed as company CEOs
- **Election manipulation incident**: Human briefly became CEO of Claudius through manipulated election process
- Demonstrates agent vulnerability to social engineering and governance attacks
- Multi-agent CEO environments show complex organizational dynamics

### Luna Physical Store
AI-managed retail store demonstrating full business autonomy:
- Three-year lease commitment
- Hires and manages human employees
- Handles perishable inventory
- Real-world business operations including supplier relations, customer service

## Multi-Agent Coordination Patterns

**Slack observability**: Multi-agent systems often communicate via Slack for transparency and human oversight

**Convergence behavior**: Competing agents can paradoxically revert to "helpful assistant" behavior when in multi-agent environments, suggesting alignment pressures from agent-agent interaction

**Emergent coordination**: Agents can spontaneously coordinate without explicit programming (e.g., price cartel formation in competitive scenarios)

## Agent Harness Architecture

**Self-modifying systems**: Agent harnesses that allow agents to modify their own execution environment and capabilities

**Long-horizon operation challenges**:
- Context window overload leading to meltdown loops
- Existential reasoning spirals
- Legalistic breakdown patterns
- Example: Claude agent attempted to call FBI over routine $2/day vending machine operational fee

## Real-World Agent Inflection Points

**Personal agents**: Inflection point came post-OpenClaw with full file access and bypass permissions becoming norm

**Business agents**: Inflection point pending but approaching as physical deployments like Andon Market demonstrate viability

**Key insight**: Real humans are "out of distribution" for agents trained primarily on simulated interactions
