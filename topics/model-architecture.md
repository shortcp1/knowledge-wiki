# Model Architecture

## Meta Muse Spark Architecture (April 2026)

### Strategic Pivot
- **First closed model from Meta**: Departure from open-weights Llama strategy
- **Origin**: First product from Meta's nine-month-old Superintelligence Labs (formed June 2025 after Llama 4 benchmark contamination allegations)
- **Architecture details**: Undisclosed (parameter count, specific architecture, training data/methods not revealed)

### Training Approach Redesign
- **Efficiency claim**: Matches Llama 4 Maverick capabilities with "over an order of magnitude less processing" for training
- **Components reworked**: Pretraining approach, model architecture, optimization, data curation
- **Thought compression**: Post-training RL that penalizes excessive reasoning tokens
  - Process: Model improves by reasoning longer → learns to compress reasoning → extends reasoning for further improvement
- **Domain-specific investment**: 1,000+ physicians enlisted to curate health training data

### Reasoning Modes
- **Three modes**: Instant, Thinking, Contemplating
- **Contemplating mode architecture**: Multi-agent parallel processing rather than single chain of thought
  - Agents "propose solutions, refine them, and aggregate results in parallel"
  - Comparable latency to single-chain reasoning with better performance
- **Performance variance by mode**: Significant (e.g., 39.9% vs 58% on Humanity's Last Exam between Thinking and Contemplating)

### Multimodal Capabilities
- **Native multimodal reasoning**: Text, image, speech input (up to 262,000 tokens context)
- **Tool use and multi-agent orchestration**: Built-in capabilities
- **Design framing**: "Architectural redesign" Meta plans to scale to larger models

### Known Performance Characteristics
- **Strengths**: Multimodal reasoning (CharXiv), health reasoning (HealthBench Hard), token efficiency, agentic browsing (DeepSearchQA)
- **Acknowledged weaknesses**: Coding tasks, general agentic work
- **Token efficiency**: ~59M tokens for Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index vs ~116M-158M for competitors
