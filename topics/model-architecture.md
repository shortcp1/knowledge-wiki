## Training Data Composition Effects

**Empirical finding**: LLM training on web-scraped data (e.g., Common Crawl subsets like CulturaX) encodes content frequency distributions from source material. Models demonstrably memorize high-frequency sequences (3-5% near-reproduction rate for top 1,000 most-repeated 20-word strings vs. near-0% baseline).

**Implication for model behavior**: Content that appears frequently in training data—regardless of veracity or representativeness—becomes more likely to be reproduced or reflected in model outputs. In jurisdictions with limited media diversity, this creates systematic biases correlated with information control policies.
