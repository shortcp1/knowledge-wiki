## Anthropic Fable/Mythos Generation (June 2026)

### Generational Characteristics

**Subjective Performance Assessment** (Thompson, June 2026): 
- Fable 5 reportedly made other frontier models (GPT 5.5, Opus 4.8) "feel small and dumb" in comparison
- Similar subjective leap previously observed only with GPT-4 and Grok 4, both representing new generations in base model size and complexity
- Assessment: Fable/Mythos likely downstream of a new pre-train, representing "the first of a new generation"
- Note: Increasingly difficult to objectively evaluate models except for coding performance; assessment based on subjective interaction quality

### Cybersecurity Capabilities

**Advanced Vulnerability Detection**: Mythos Preview (April 2026) was initially withheld from public release specifically due to "advanced cybersecurity capabilities" - ability to identify and exploit security issues at levels beyond previous models.

**Guardrail Effectiveness**: Fable 5 released with safety guardrails, but jailbroken shortly after public release, demonstrating the persistent challenge of maintaining restrictions on publicly-released models.
