# AI in Operations

Covers AI in internal operations: workflow automation, process mining, IT service management, supply chain optimization, and document processing. Tracks both standalone operations AI tools and AI features in ERP/BPM platforms.

Key questions tracked: Where is AI actually replacing manual processes end-to-end vs. augmenting? What is the integration cost of AI operations tools in legacy environments?
