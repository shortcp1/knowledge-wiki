# AI in Insurance & Claims

Covers AI in underwriting, claims processing, fraud detection, and actuarial modeling. Tracks both InsurTech pure-plays and AI deployments by legacy carriers. Includes Verisk and adjacent data vendors.

Key questions tracked: What is the accuracy profile of AI claims adjudication vs. human review? Where is AI actually reducing loss ratios? How are regulators responding to AI-driven pricing?
