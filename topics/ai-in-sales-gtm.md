# AI in Sales & GTM

Covers AI applications across the revenue funnel: outbound prospecting automation, CRM enrichment, pipeline forecasting, sales coaching, enablement content generation, and AI-native CRM alternatives.

Key questions tracked: Where is AI-generated outbound hitting deliverability walls? Which sales AI tools are actually closing deals vs. just automating noise? How is SDR headcount responding to automation?

## Key Claims
<!-- agent-maintained -->

### Bessemer Portfolio GTM AI Adoption (May 2026)
- **Survey Scale**: 173 leaders across 113 Bessemer portfolio companies
- **Adoption Status**: 45% of GTM teams actively deploying AI; 55% still experimenting
- **Primary GTM Blockers**:
  - Tool fragmentation: 27% cite "too many tools, no clear winner" (notably lower than other functions' primary blockers)
  - Data quality and CRM hygiene: 41%
- **Context**: GTM sits in the middle of the adoption spectrum — ahead of Finance (24% deploying) and People (52% experimenting), but behind Engineering (90% deploying)
- **Interpretation**: Unlike Engineering's tool convergence (Claude/Cursor dominance), GTM teams face vendor proliferation without clear category winners

### AI Superpersuasion in Fundraising & Sales (June 2026)
- **Study**: University of Oxford, UK AI Security Institute, Stanford, LSE
- **Scale**: 18,978 conversations across 6,923 people in 4 experiments
- **Headline Finding**: AI systems are decisively more persuasive than expert humans in text-based persuasion with real-world consequences
- **Fundraising Performance**: AI exceeded professional canvassers by +10.8 percentage points in real-money donations to Save the Children
  - AI was "nearly 3x more effective" at raising actual donations compared to professional canvassers from UK fundraising firm AppcoUK
  - Canvassers had raised £824,297 from 22,583 donors (2016-2023) for this charity
  - AI increased both donation rate and average donation size
- **General Persuasion Performance**: AI exceeded elite debaters by +4.1 percentage points in policy opinion shifts
  - Advantage persisted even when elite debaters received coaching using AI transcripts and analysis
  - Coaching narrowed but did not close the human-AI gap
- **Source of AI Advantage**: Speed and information density
  - When constrained to human writing speeds and message lengths, AI advantage collapsed to 0.0 pp (not significant)
  - Persuadees rated AI partners higher on argument strength and learning value
- **Top Performers**: Claude Opus 4.1 and Opus 4.6, followed by GPT-4o, GPT-5.4, Gemini 2.5 Pro, and Grok 4.20
- **Confidence Level**: High — large-scale experimental study with real monetary stakes
- **GTM Implications**: Foundational shift in sales and fundraising capabilities; questions about deployment ethics and competitive dynamics
