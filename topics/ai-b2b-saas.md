# AI in B2B SaaS

Tracks how AI is reshaping B2B SaaS business models, competitive dynamics, and growth metrics: NRR trends, the AI feature premium, displacement of point solutions by AI-native competitors, and benchmarks for AI-era SaaS companies.

Key questions tracked: Which SaaS categories are most vulnerable to AI disruption? How is AI changing the sales motion for SaaS? What are the new unit economics benchmarks for AI-heavy SaaS?

## Key Claims

### AI Economy Demand-Side Visibility Challenges (June 2025)
- **Revenue transparency problem**: Demand-side AI revenues harder to measure than supply-side due to private company concentration (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, ElevenLabs, hundreds of others have no legal disclosure requirements)
- **Hyperscaler disclosure gaps**: Public hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft) don't consistently disclose AI segment revenues despite serving AI models
- **Market measurement approach**: Requires triangulation of public statements, supplier data, customer reports, and "well-reported leaks" with confidence scoring (Exponential View methodology, June 2025)

### Enterprise AI Spending Patterns (June 2025)
- **Professional services multiplier**: Fortune 500 AI investments include significant non-product spend on systems integration and implementation services (excluded from direct AI economy measurements)
- **Deployment stage**: Enterprises moving beyond pilots but still in "early stages of scaling and deepening" adoption

### Application Layer Moat Dynamics

## Public SaaS Market Performance by Category (1Y as of June 2026)

**Source**: Tomasz Tunguz analysis of 87 public SaaS & platform companies

### Winner Categories: AI Stack Components

**Infrastructure & Dev Tools** (+68.5% 1Y)
- **Constituents**: DigitalOcean, JFrog, Datadog, MongoDB, Cloudflare, Snowflake, Confluent (13 names total)
- **Metrics**: 21.4% revenue growth, 10.0x EV/Sales
- **Market thesis**: Combines agent compute, AI data stack, and developer tooling
- **Top performer**: DigitalOcean +430% 1Y
- Other +50% performers: Datadog, Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet

**Security** (+17.6% 1Y)
- **Constituents**: Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet, CrowdStrike, Okta (8 names total)
- **Metrics**: 24.1% revenue growth (fastest in sample), 11.6x EV/Sales (highest multiple in sample)
- **Market thesis**: "AI is expanding both the attack surface & the defense surface"
- **Investment rationale**: CIOs prioritize "the line item that holds the perimeter"

### Neutral/Mixed Categories

**AI & Mega-cap Platforms** (-5.9% 1Y)
- **Constituents**: Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Meta, Oracle, Palantir (9 names total)
- **Metrics**: 21.5% revenue growth
- **Note**: "Growth & profitability are not enough" - despite strong fundamentals, category underperforms

**Communications & Collaboration** (-6.6% 1Y)
- **Constituents**: 9 names total
- **Metrics**: 8.2% revenue growth, 2.4x EV/Sales
- **Exception proving the rule**: Twilio +62% 1Y because "AI agents send messages, & every agent needs a phone number" - AI-adjacent positioning within losing sector drives outperformance

### Loser Category: Seat-Based Applications

**Business Applications** (-36.2% 1Y)
- **Constituents**: Salesforce, Workday, ServiceNow, Monday.com, HubSpot, Atlassian (48 names total - "most of public SaaS")
- **Metrics**: 12.5% revenue growth, 3.4x EV/Sales
- **Market thesis**: "Seat-priced layer most exposed to agent substitution"
- **Specific struggles**: Monday.com, HubSpot, Atlassian noted as underperformers

## The Agent Substitution Thesis

### Salesforce Case Study (Sept 2025)
**Source**: Marc Benioff, The Logan Bartlett Show, September 2, 2025

- **Headcount reduction**: 9,000 → ~5,000 support staff
- **Agentforce adoption**: Handles ~50% of Salesforce's customer interactions
- **Cost impact**: Support costs down 17%
- **Market signal**: "Every CIO running Service Cloud just heard that on the same podcast"

### CIO Buying Pattern (June 2026)

**Investment thesis**: "Fund the AI stack, cut everything else"

**Key insight**: Growth rate does not separate winners from losers
- Infrastructure, Security, and mega-caps all grow ~21%
- **What separates them is category**: "The market pays premium multiples for sectors CIOs believe are necessary during AI, & punishes horizontal application software where AI threatens the seat-based model"

### Token-Path Positioning

**Framework**: "Is your product a payment processor or a software business?"
- **Winners sit on the token path**: Twilio, DigitalOcean, Cloudflare, MongoDB benefit because:
  - AI agents send messages (Twilio)
  - AI agents run on compute (DigitalOcean)
  - AI agents route through edges (Cloudflare)
  - AI agents query data (MongoDB)
