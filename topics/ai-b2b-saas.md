# AI in B2B SaaS

Tracks how AI is reshaping B2B SaaS business models, competitive dynamics, and growth metrics: NRR trends, the AI feature premium, displacement of point solutions by AI-native competitors, and benchmarks for AI-era SaaS companies.

Key questions tracked: Which SaaS categories are most vulnerable to AI disruption? How is AI changing the sales motion for SaaS? What are the new unit economics benchmarks for AI-heavy SaaS?

## Key Claims

### AI Economy Demand-Side Visibility Challenges (June 2025)
- **Revenue transparency problem**: Demand-side AI revenues harder to measure than supply-side due to private company concentration (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, ElevenLabs, hundreds of others have no legal disclosure requirements)
- **Hyperscaler disclosure gaps**: Public hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft) don't consistently disclose AI segment revenues despite serving AI models
- **Market measurement approach**: Requires triangulation of public statements, supplier data, customer reports, and "well-reported leaks" with confidence scoring (Exponential View methodology, June 2025)

### Enterprise AI Spending Patterns (June 2025)
- **Professional services multiplier**: Fortune 500 AI investments include significant non-product spend on systems integration and implementation services (excluded from direct AI economy measurements)
- **Deployment stage**: Enterprises moving beyond pilots but still in "early stages of scaling and deepening" adoption
