# AI in Professional Services

Tracks AI in consulting, legal, accounting, and other professional services: workflow automation, knowledge management, client deliverable generation, and the business model implications of AI-driven efficiency in billing-by-the-hour businesses.

Key questions tracked: How are Big 4 firms deploying AI internally vs. selling AI services? What is the billing model transition path from time-based to outcome-based? Where is AI creating liability risk in professional opinions?
