## White House AI Commitments (July 21, 2023)

Major AI labs including Anthropic committed to "internal and external security testing of [their] AI systems" to guard against "some of the most significant sources of AI risks, such as biosecurity and cybersecurity."

**Policy Context**: Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei highlighted national security-relevant AI capabilities in Senate testimony prior to these commitments.

## Frontier Threats Red Teaming for National Security

**Specialized Red Teaming**: Distinct from general crowdworker-based red teaming (previously reported by Anthropic). Focuses on capabilities relevant to national security such as biosecurity and cybersecurity.

**Risk Monitoring Framework**:
- Researchers have called for measurement and monitoring of national security-relevant capabilities
- Evidence of risks documented in academic papers
- Anticipation that AI models could "eventually obtain capabilities in areas relevant to national security"

### Biology/Biosecurity Findings (July 2023)

**Threat Scope**: Models evaluated for ability to output harmful biological information, including:
- Designing biological weapons
- Acquiring biological weapons
- Other sensitive biological information (details highly sensitive)

**Assessment Conclusions**:
- Models "might soon present risks to national security, if unmitigated"
- Current risk baseline: small but "growing relatively fast"
- Near-term actualization timeline: 2-3 years from July 2023
- **Mitigation availability**: "There are mitigations to substantially reduce these risks"

**Operational Approach**:
- Use of secure, bespoke interfaces without public deployment safeguards
- Trust and safety monitoring/enforcement tools disabled during testing
- Findings shared with government, labs, and stakeholders

### Implementation Strategy

**Scaling Plans**: Anthropic scaling up frontier threats red teaming to "reliably identify risks and build mitigations."

**Ecosystem Development**: Desire for "more independent groups doing this work" beyond individual labs.

**Dual Benefits**: Improving frontier threats red teaming contributes to both immediate safety and long-term AI safety goals.
