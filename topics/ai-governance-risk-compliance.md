## Intent Verification as Governance Challenge (July 2026)

### The Observable Decision Problem
- **Core Governance Gap**: Agentic commerce creates "invisible decision-making" - transactions remain visible but decision trails become opaque
- **Historical Foundation Breaking**: Financial services governance built on assumption of "visible human decision" at critical moments (clicking Buy, signing agreements, tapping Confirm)
- **What Made Traditional Governance Work**: Observable consent moments served as:
  - Evidence customer understood the decision
  - Proof customer accepted consequences
  - Demonstration of intent
  - Foundation for fraud detection (normal vs. suspicious behavior)
  - Signal for risk assessment (application behavior inferring commitment)
  - Basis for dispute resolution (clear consent identification)

### Industry Response Pattern: Decision Trail Reconstruction
- **Governance Reconstruction Focus Areas**:
  - **Intent verification systems**: Capturing agent decision rationale
  - **Context capture**: Recording environmental factors influencing agent decisions
  - **Governance frameworks**: New structures for agent oversight
  - **Permission systems**: Granular control over agent actions
  - **Explainability requirements**: Making agent reasoning auditable
  - **Accountability mechanisms**: Assigning responsibility for agent actions

### Know Your Agent (KYA) Framework
- **Pattern**: BNPL providers exploring KYC-like approaches for AI agents
- **Rationale**: Need to verify agent identity, authority, and intent before extending credit
- **Governance Parallel**: Applying existing identity/verification frameworks to non-human actors

### Cross-Industry Governance Convergence
- **Observation**: "Companies across financial services suddenly working on similar problems, even if using different language"
- **Implication**: Intent verification becoming universal governance requirement across:
  - Lending (risk evaluation)
  - Payments (fraud detection)
  - BNPL (credit decisions)
  - Merchant services (transaction validation)

### Generalizability of Governance Challenge
**Pattern applies to any regulated industry requiring proof of informed consent**:
- **Healthcare**: Treatment authorization, prescription fulfillment
- **Legal**: Contract acceptance, power of attorney verification
- **Insurance**: Claims submission, policy changes
- **Real estate**: Offer submission, document signing
- **Government services**: Benefit applications, regulatory filings

### Key Governance Architecture Shift
- **From**: Monitoring human actions at transaction points
- **To**: Reconstructing and auditing agent decision chains
- **Challenge**: Traditional compliance tools (fraud detection, behavior analysis, dispute resolution) designed for observable human decisions
- **Required Innovation**: New governance infrastructure capturing what AI removes from view
