# AI in HR & Talent

Covers AI in recruiting (screening, sourcing, interview prep), onboarding automation, performance management, workforce planning, and HR service delivery. Tracks compliance risks around AI in employment decisions.

Key questions tracked: Which hiring stages are seeing the highest AI adoption? What are the legal risks of AI-assisted hiring in different jurisdictions? How is AI changing L&D and upskilling?
