# Build vs. Buy Enterprise AI

Tracks decision frameworks and real-world outcomes for enterprises choosing between building proprietary AI capabilities vs. buying point solutions vs. using foundation model APIs directly.

Key questions tracked: What is the true total cost of a build vs. buy decision at different scales? How is the vendor landscape consolidating? When does proprietary fine-tuning justify the cost?

## Key Claims

### Market Structure Predictions
- **Software Value Compression Thesis (O'Laughlin, Jan 2026)**: Claims that traditional SaaS business models, especially seat-based licensing, face structural challenges due to AI capabilities:
  - Multiple compression in SaaS company valuations characterized as "painful and will persist"
  - Described as shift from "SaaS is eating the world" (2012) to "SaaS is screwed" (2026)
  - Prediction: Software will become "an extension of hardware" rather than commanding independent value
  - Argument: Non-deterministic AI agents will handle ephemeral computation while trad

### AI Implementation Layer Emergence (May 2026)
- **Strategic Pattern**: Foundation model companies discovering that model capability alone is insufficient for enterprise adoption
  - Anthropic acquiring Fractional AI (implementation services for midsize/PE-backed firms)
  - Building enterprise consulting venture backed by Blackstone, Apollo, General Atlantic
  - **Key Insight**: "Enterprise adoption depends as much on implementation, integration, and operational support as it does on the underlying technology itself"
  - Pattern mirrors earlier cloud software industry learning: distribution and deployment expertise matters as much as core technology
- **Financial Context (Anthropic)**: Revenue reportedly surging from $4.8B (Q1 2026) to $10.9B (Q2 2026) while compute costs decline as percentage of revenue - creating room for aggressive enterprise expansion
- **Competitive Implication**: Model providers increasingly competing on deployment and workflow expertise, not just model performance

### Workflow Orchestration as Strategic Layer
- **Coupa Acquiring Tonkean (May 2026)**: Spend management platform acquiring AI-native workflow orchestration company
  - Tonkean enables natural language requests → AI agent coordination of approvals/workflows across existing enterprise systems
  - **Key Capability**: Orchestration without requiring infrastructure replacement
  - **Strategic Thesis**: Workflow orchestration becoming critical enterprise AI capability
- **Implication**: Companies buying the operational layer where AI systems will execute, not just the AI capabilities themselves
