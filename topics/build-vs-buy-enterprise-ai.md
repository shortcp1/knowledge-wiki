# Build vs. Buy Enterprise AI

Tracks decision frameworks and real-world outcomes for enterprises choosing between building proprietary AI capabilities vs. buying point solutions vs. using foundation model APIs directly.

Key questions tracked: What is the true total cost of a build vs. buy decision at different scales? How is the vendor landscape consolidating? When does proprietary fine-tuning justify the cost?

## Key Claims
<!-- agent-maintained -->

### Market Structure Predictions
- **Software Value Compression Thesis (O'Laughlin, Jan 2026)**: Claims that traditional SaaS business models, especially seat-based licensing, face structural challenges due to AI capabilities:
  - Multiple compression in SaaS company valuations characterized as "painful and will persist"
  - Described as shift from "SaaS is eating the world" (2012) to "SaaS is screwed" (2026)
  - Prediction

### Willingness to Pay for Intelligence (Lambert, Jun 2026)
- **Premium for Best Intelligence**: First clear signal that users will pay substantial premiums for superior intelligence in coding agents
  - Opus 4.5 and Codex 5.2 identified as "threshold" models where users became unwilling to downgrade
  - Claim: Users in coding-intensive knowledge work "will always pay more for the best rather than settle for good enough"
  - Example stated willingness to pay: $2000/month for current tools (from technical user)
  - Compared to consumer iPhone purchasing behavior: marginal cost savings not worth performance/integration sacrifice
  - **Caveat**: Acknowledged that many companies are forcing agent adoption on users who get "very little out of them in their current form"

### Open vs. Closed Model Economics (Lambert, Jun 2026)
- **Closed Lab Structural Advantages**: Claims closed labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, expected Google) will maintain efficiency advantage through:
  - "Massive returns on integration" of hardware and software vs. open models designed for diverse serving situations
  - Ability to optimize in "every direction": intelligence per cost, utility per second, utility per watt
  - Access to mass capital investment in talent, data, and compute
  - Integration of model weights, harnesses, tools, and serving infrastructure

- **API Business Decay Prediction**: Closed labs predicted to protect best models by:
  - Rolling out frontier models later to APIs
  - Protecting token supply
  - Avoiding distillation
  - Focusing on higher-margin use cases
  - Timeline: "clearly visible in 5-10 year timelines"

- **Near-term Market Distortions**: Acknowledged that current pricing/margins are distorted by:
  - Supply-limited compute (rapid datacenter buildout)
  - Mass subsidization of tokens through continued investment in AI companies

### Long-term Market Structure
- **Frontier Lab Oligopoly (Lambert, Jun 2026)**: Prediction that frontier labs will resemble "Apple + Microsoft" hybrid:
  - Apple-like: Integrated, extremely hard to replicate technology
  - Microsoft-like: High-leverage subscriptions across economy
  - Valuation prediction: OpenAI and Anthropic at $2-10T range in 5-10 years
  - Market structure: Oligopoly resembling current cloud market
  - **Key assumption**: "No walls in progress" for intelligence improvements
